# SECV_Tlajomulco

Code and derived data for the book chapter **"Los límites de la mirada. Archivo fotogramétrico y transformación socioambiental"** (Ana Paula Montes Ruiz and Emilio José Bermeo Donoso), forthcoming in *El punto ciego*, Seminario Permanente de Estudios de Cultura Visual, CEIICH-UNAM.

The chapter reads INEGI's aerial photography archive of Tlajomulco de Zúñiga (Jalisco, Mexico) through two operations:

1. **Archive coverage.** Counts and flight-line geometry from the flight index of INEGI's Sistema de Información de Índices de Vuelos (SIIV), queried on 7 July 2026 (2,074 records).
2. **Cartographic regression.** Georeferencing of three aerial photographs (February 1971 and February 1993 over the airport corridor; February 1971 over the Presa La Playa), with prediction errors reported against the reference base (Esri World Imagery Wayback, version of 5 August 2026).

## Status

Version 0.1 reserves the DOI. The full contents (scripts, derived tables, ground control point files, the 1971 outline of the Presa La Playa basin, summarized JRC Global Surface Water and Landsat MSS series, and the table of validated figures) will be added before December 2026.
